## UP-WEIGHT

- Pediatric, adolescent, or youth samples.
- Intensive longitudinal designs: EMA, ESM, passive sensing, wearables, measurement burst.
- Machine learning and dynamical systems contributions, especially technical advances.

- Pharmacology, immunology, or molecular work with no link to psychiatric phenotypes.
  Genetics, neuroimaging, and neurobiology are wanted, but route them to `adjacent` and

  Score these below 0.40 unless the review is a substantive methodological synthesis.
